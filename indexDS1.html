Home Page

Welcome to the official website of OB Montessori Center! We are a school that believes every student can learn and grow in their own way. Our teachers guide each student so they can discover what they are good at and become the best version of themselves. Explore our website to learn more about our school, how to enroll and how to contact us.

About Us

OB Montessori Center is a school that follows the Montessori way of teaching. This means students learn by doing things, working at their own pace and asking questions. We want our students to not only be smart but also to have good values. Our school has caring teachers, clean classrooms and many activities where students can show their talents in academics, arts and sports.
